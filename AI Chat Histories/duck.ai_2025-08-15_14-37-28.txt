- Reduced equator-to-pole temperature gradients

In contrast, the pre-Industrial climate featured more pronounced seasonal and latitudinal temperature differences, with established polar ice caps and cooler global averages.

By the 18th century, volcanism was far less influential on global scales, and stable sea levels helped maintain consistent ocean–atmosphere carbon exchange rates.
